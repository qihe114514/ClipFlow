# ClipFlow Promo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Dark fluid technology for a practical Android media utility. Use real ClipFlow interface captures as the visual anchor, framed by translucent glass surfaces, soft blue-purple light, and restrained liquid motion. The result should feel fast and precise, with enough contrast for small mobile-screen details to remain readable.

## Colors

- `#0B0D14` - dark canvas
- `#6B9DFC` - primary ClipFlow blue
- `#9B7BFC` - secondary ClipFlow purple
- `#00D4D4` - Douyin accent
- `#FF3B5C` - Xiaohongshu accent

## Typography

- System sans-serif stack
- Bold or semibold display text; regular body text
- No condensed or decorative display fonts

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use a generic blue gradient as the entire background.
- Do not invent product screens that contradict the Android app.
- Do not stretch or crop device UI to hide content.
- Do not use oversized text that covers the interface capture.
- Do not show private links, account data, cookies, or tokens.
